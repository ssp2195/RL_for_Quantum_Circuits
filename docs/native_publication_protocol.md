# Native publication implementation and claim boundary

Baseline: `9f811ca5a5f5ca10389032a0045270d469b64a56`, branch
`hybrid-frontier-restored-v1`. Historical phase-study data and manuscripts are
preserved, not re-labelled or combined with native results.

## Six requested pieces

1. **Defensible contribution.** The new manuscript describes an auditable native
   search/exact-evidence interface and its controlled empirical assessment. It
   compares relevant exact-synthesis, Pauli-network RL, AlphaTensor and learned
   branch-and-bound literature. It explicitly does not claim that combining
   learning and verification, determinant obstructions, or elementary circuit
   identities is new. Performance claims come from the new campaign only.
2. **Decision-matched training.** Specification-only mixed-basis, non-Clifford,
   entangling native targets and crossed clean-workspace training replace the
   earlier inadequate phase-only exercise. All six continuation families receive
   updates. Five completed frozen production seeds are separate from a shorter,
   fully completed regression-test training schedule. No generating word, macro
   scaffold, phase obligation, or auditor witness enters policy training.
3. **Attributable evaluation.** Native gate-cap tightening is discovery-only in
   primary evaluation. Audits cannot rescue a failed run. Equal-edge and equal-
   wall comparisons are distinct; the latter does not use a small binding edge
   limit. Global goal-on-pop uniform-cost and cold-table native meet-in-the-middle
   are independent nonlearned controls. Both successes and failures are retained,
   late witnesses are archived but not credited, and target-cluster analysis
   avoids treating seeds/timing repetitions as independent logical targets.
4. **Resource/ancilla trade-offs.** A crossed six-target clean-width campaign and
   native controlled-S discovery are separate from exact known reference replay.
   Known determinant exclusions and exact one-clean-wire QFT-3/C^3X witnesses
   establish meaningful exact clean-width minima. Their provenance is not RL.
   The original 43 challenge contracts remain unchanged and unresolved results
   are not erased or converted into impossibility claims.
5. **Manuscript theorems.** Hybrid semantic invariants, resource monotonicity,
   coverage, exact closed-cover checking, lexicographic minima, clean-width
   minima, scoring costs and potential telescoping have explicit hypotheses.
   The additional exact ring verifier is run, not inferred from a numerical
   checker. Shared native symbolic algebra remains a trusted component.
6. **Application and reproducibility.** Coherent amplitude amplification uses
   generated native oracles when found. A small new two-input threshold-network
   calibration complements, but does not replace, the unchanged three-input BNN
   challenge. Locked protocol/source files, training logs, checkpoints, raw
   trials, exact checks, references, diagnostics, plots and data-generated
   manuscript statements have one provenance chain.

## Invariants and controlled amendments

Every actual native discovery state remains `(G,Theta,R,phi,rho)`. No PhaseState
is used. NativeProblem and its gate/resource contract are unchanged. The only
amendment to NativeSearch is an optional, explicitly named inner-response
ablation plus training diagnostics; the existing `child` default is preserved.
StudySearch supplies remaining work according to the experimentally selected
budget axis. StudyPolicy preserves 20 outer and 24 inner coordinates; the
no-structure ablation masks coordinates in both prediction and updates.

The candidate panel has size 32 and global fairness period 32. The whole frontier
remains available. A separate fixed-frontier diagnostic distinguishes arithmetic
scoring cost from the policy change caused by restricting candidate selection.

## Primary protocol

Thirty test logical targets: eight one-qubit, sixteen two-qubit and six three-
qubit transformations. The generating native words have lengths 3--7 depending
on stratum. These lengths are upper bounds, not asserted minima. Targets are
filtered only by syntactic mixed-axis/connected structure, actual operator
entanglement, and exact phase/adjoint/wire-permutation orbit de-duplication. No
measured search success determines inclusion. Thirty-six training and twelve
validation targets are disjoint; twenty-four regression targets are additional.

Five production seeds (11,19,23,31,47) receive 64/96/24 training episodes. The
128-edge training allowance and 1.5-second safety limit are explicit. Learning
rate selection considers .003/.01/.03 using only validation seed 101. All fitting,
including three separately retrained ablations on three seeds, is bounded by
1,800 CPU seconds in aggregate. Stopping at that cap is a reported failure of the
campaign, not a claim that training completed.

Primary budgets: 128 and 1024 attempted native edges; separately 0.2 and 1.0 wall
seconds. Record ceiling 30,000; time-based runs allow ten million attempted edges
so the edge limit should not bind. Guard-limited runs are reported rather than
called equal-edge completions. Timed jobs are repeated twice and interleaved.

The primary endpoint is Q=success*(Gcap+1-G)/(Gcap+1) at one second. Every timely
feasible circuit has positive Q and unresolved runs have zero Q. Conditional gate
counts, actual savings from the generating specification, success and time are
also shown. A paired bootstrap averages seeds/repetitions within a logical target
before resampling targets. Other budgets/ablations are descriptive; intervals
are not multiplicity-adjusted. This is a predeclared artifact protocol, not a
claim of an external preregistration.

## Proof and diagnostic separation

The fixed post-campaign proof sample contains the first eight one-qubit test
cases and the first four two-qubit test cases. It uses 15-second cooperative
optimization ledgers, gate-first lexicographic objectives, and no adoption of
audit-found witnesses. Unfinished gaps remain upper bounds/unknown. Exact receipt
checking is an additional measured verification, not a numerical label change.

The bounded-continuation diagnostic reads withheld constructor prefixes only
AFTER training and primary evaluation. It tests two-step completions, verifies
found witnesses exactly, and does not label an unresolved continuation globally
bad or impossible. These probes are off-policy and must not be described as the
actual visitation distribution of the trained policy.

## Claims not established by implementing this protocol

A trained-policy advantage, a novel gate identity, general exact optimality,
large-register scalability, and a quantum advantage for realistic BNN validation
are not guaranteed. The generated manuscript states the observed outcome,
including a negative or statistically unresolved learning comparison. A complete
software artifact is not a substitute for evidence supporting a positive claim.
