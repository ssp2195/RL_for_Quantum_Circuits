\section{Conclusion}

This work presents a standalone exact-synthesis architecture for clean-ancilla Boolean verification phase oracles.  A predicate is supplied by its exact Boolean semantics rather than by a quantum circuit.  Outer linear SARSA selects a persistent evaluator-search record, inner disjoint LinUCB ranks the record's pending reversible continuations, and fair deferred expansion invokes a deterministic mapping/Pareto pipeline only on the selected batch.  A certified evaluator is lowered to native Clifford+$T$ through a persistent DAG, forward--inverse Clifford tableau, ordered Pauli rotations, and strengthened canonicalization.  The universal transformation $U_g^\dagger Z_fU_g$ then produces the phase oracle, which is accepted only after independent exact-isometry and clean-workspace certification.

For the three-input BNN robustness predicate, the hierarchy discovers a six-macro evaluator computing $x_1\bar x_2\bar x_3$ and restoring one clean work qubit.  Native lowering gives a 48-gate evaluator and a 98-gate phase oracle with 42 $T/T^\dagger$ gates, 42 CNOTs, exact-isometry error $1.22\times10^{-15}$, and leakage $3.26\times10^{-32}$.  Fifty-nine tests pass.  The exact target-potential control remains faster on this small target, so the evidence establishes correctness and generation feasibility rather than universal scheduling superiority.

The restricted 35-gate QFT-3 result remains a complementary test of clean-ancilla controlled-phase lowering; its bounded unrestricted-search failure is reported separately.  The next scalable step is an exact Boolean intermediate representation with deterministic reversible pebbling and a certified Pareto library of small blocks.  The present hierarchy can then remain the local exact synthesizer, preserving the central rule that learning allocates search effort while exact mathematics determines semantics, pruning validity, workspace restoration, and final correctness.
