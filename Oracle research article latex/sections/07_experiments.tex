\section{Experimental Methodology}
\label{sec:experiments}

\subsection{Implementation and regression protocol}

The implementation is written in Python and NumPy.  The native semantic engine stores a persistent circuit DAG, forward--inverse Clifford tableau, ordered Pauli rotations, exact phase coordinate, and monotone resource state.  Every macro selected by the oracle search is lowered through this engine with partial-order reduction disabled during final witness replay, ensuring that the certified chronological circuit is exactly the circuit returned by the search.

The complete test suite contains 59 passing tests.  The inherited tests cover native gate matrices, tableau identities, Pauli transport, strengthened canonicalization, duplicate and Pareto pruning, mixed-gate deferred expansion, clean and borrowed ancilla contracts, exact/projective phase modes, and the restricted QFT-3 construction.  Seven oracle tests add truth-table semantics, target-independent macro enumeration, exact workspace restoration, native lowering, exact phase marking, common-suffix mapping invariance, and end-to-end hierarchical generation.

The bounded canonicalization audit enumerates 3906 one-qubit prefixes through depth five and 1885 two-qubit prefixes through depth three.  An independent dense projective fingerprint is used only as a differential-test oracle.  The strengthened key reduces unresolved duplicate excess by approximately $51\%$ and $50\%$, respectively, with no observed false merge in the audited samples.

\subsection{Predicate curriculum and frozen policies}

The outer and inner linear policies are trained on the first four targets of a target-independent curriculum:
\begin{equation}
 x_1,\qquad \bar x_1,\qquad x_1\oplus x_2,\qquad x_1x_2.
\end{equation}
The held-out BNN predicate is not used during policy updates.  Outer SARSA receives two episodes with at most 32 allocations and batches of two projected-distance-ordered macros.  LinUCB then receives four one-edge episodes while the outer policy is fixed.  The learning rate is $10^{-4}$, the LinUCB confidence coefficient is $0.05$, and the ridge regularization is $1000$.  Frozen evaluation uses no $\varepsilon$ exploration and no UCB optimism.

The BNN run permits at most 1000 outer allocations, 4000 exact macro attempts, seven macros in a witness, a batch size of four, and 12 wall-clock seconds.  The corresponding native budget is fixed by register width rather than by the discovered witness:
\begin{equation}
B_T=35,
\quad B_{\CNOT}=48,
\quad B_g=96,
\quad B_D=88
\end{equation}
for the evaluator.  The phase wrapper doubles the evaluator budget and adds the two native $S$ gates implementing $Z_f=S_f^2$.

\subsection{BNN verification-oracle target}

The source verification example has reference input $000$, Hamming radius one, and perturbation set
\begin{equation}
\{000,100,010,001\}.
\end{equation}
Exactly $100$ changes the BNN output.  The synthesis benchmark adopts the exact three-bit phase oracle in \eqref{eq:bnn-marked-oracle}, equivalently
\begin{equation}
g(x_1,x_2,x_3)=x_1\bar x_2\bar x_3.
\end{equation}
Qubits $q_0,q_1,q_2$ store $x_1,x_2,x_3$; $q_3$ is the predicate flag and $q_4$ is one clean work qubit.  The evaluator and final phase oracle are checked on all eight data inputs, not only on the four elements of the Hamming ball.

\subsection{Control schedulers}

Three frozen search procedures use the same macro grammar, mapping transition, resource budgets, archive, native lowering, and final certifier:
\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{Hierarchical SARSA--LinUCB}: outer records are ranked by the frozen SARSA score and pending macros by frozen LinUCB posterior means.
\item \textbf{Deferred fixed order}: the same frozen outer SARSA policy is retained, but pending macros are processed in deterministic token order.
\item \textbf{Exact target potential}: the outer record and its macros are ordered directly by the exact mapping distance in Section~\ref{sec:boolean-oracle}.
\end{enumerate}
All methods are run five times with frozen policies to measure implementation-level timing dispersion.  Because the searches are deterministic after policy freezing, repetitions are timing repetitions rather than independent learner seeds.

The primary feasibility endpoint is independent exact certification of the phase oracle.  Explanatory metrics are wall time, outer allocations, exact macro attempts, peak frontier size, native gate resources, exact-isometry error, and clean-workspace leakage.  The controls are included to determine whether the learning hierarchy adds value beyond deferred expansion and an exact greedy potential; they are not alternative correctness conditions.

\subsection{Restricted QFT-3 qualification}

A separate one-clean-ancilla QFT-3 experiment tests mixed Clifford+$T$ phase handling, compute--phase--uncompute, and exact isometry certification.  It is deliberately not part of the specification-level Boolean oracle search.  A verified analytical macro plan lowers Hadamards, controlled-$S$, a clean-ancilla controlled-$T$, and final bit reversal to 35 native gates with 15 $T/T^\dagger$ gates, 13 CNOTs, and depth 26.  The generator receives the fact that the target is QFT and is therefore labelled \emph{restricted}.  A generic unrestricted native search is also run for 1.5 seconds and 585 exact edges; its negative result is retained.

\subsection{Reproducibility artifacts}

The repository stores raw CSV files, JSON certificates, the complete native witness, frozen linear-policy parameters, test logs, plots, and figure-generation source.  New diagrams in this article are expressed directly in TikZ; quantitative figures are rendered by PGFPlots from the packaged CSV data.  Thus no raster illustration or generative image model is required to reproduce the article's methodological figures.
