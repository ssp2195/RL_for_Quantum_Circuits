\section{Related Work}

\subsection{Exact Clifford+$T$ synthesis and symbolic representations}
Exact Clifford+$T$ synthesis is constrained by the algebraic structure of the representable unitary matrices.  Giles and Selinger characterize multiqubit exact synthesis with local ancillas and distinguish it from the ancilla-free case~\cite{giles2013exact}; Kliuchnikov develops related exact-synthesis foundations~\cite{kliuchnikov2013synthesis}.  Clifford transformations admit compact tableau or binary symplectic representations~\cite{aaronson2004improved,bravyi2021hadamard}.  A $T$ gate can be represented, up to a tracked scalar, as a $\pi/4$ rotation about a Pauli operator transported through the Clifford frame~\cite{zhang2019optimizing,vandaele2023optimal}.  The present native state uses these exact ingredients and retains both forward and inverse Pauli actions.

The canonicalizer is deliberately sound rather than complete.  It applies signed-axis normalization, same-axis fusion, commuting reordering, and exact extraction of Clifford-valued rotations.  Independent equivalence checking remains necessary because different noncommuting rotation words can represent the same circuit without reducing to the same symbolic key~\cite{burgholzer2020advanced}.

\subsection{Reversible Boolean computation and phase oracles}
A classical Boolean computation can be embedded reversibly by preserving its input and XORing its output into a target bit.  Temporary results are erased by applying the inverse computation, a standard construction in quantum computation~\cite{nielsen2010quantum}.  For phase marking, the evaluator is placed around a Pauli-$Z$ on its output flag, yielding $U_g^\dagger Z_fU_g$.  This identity is the semantic interface between reversible logic synthesis and Grover-style amplitude amplification~\cite{grover1996fast,brassard2002amplitude}.

BNNs are attractive verification targets because their weights and activations admit exact Boolean encodings~\cite{courbariaux2016binarized,narodytska2018verifying}.  The motivating verification formulation represents a robustness violation as a Boolean predicate, constructs a compute--phase--uncompute oracle, and uses amplitude amplification or estimation to search or count violating inputs.  The present work does not claim an end-to-end quantum speedup; it addresses the exact oracle-construction problem on the paper's concrete three-input predicate.

\subsection{Learning-guided circuit and search control}
Reinforcement learning has been applied to gate-level quantum-circuit construction, transpilation, ZX-calculus rewriting, Pauli-network synthesis, and symmetry-aware Clifford reduction~\cite{kolle2024environment,kremer2024practical,riu2023zx,dubal2025pauli,yeung2026equivariant}.  Learned residual-cost estimates have also been embedded in explicit search~\cite{theissinger2026beyond}.  Our policies have a narrower role: they allocate exact search work over already admitted states and target-independent reversible continuations.

The outer decision has delayed consequences because it changes the future frontier, motivating semi-gradient SARSA~\cite{sutton2018rl}.  The inner continuation decision has an immediate observed cost and mapping effect, motivating a linear contextual bandit.  LinUCB supplies an interpretable ridge estimate and confidence bonus during training~\cite{li2010contextual,abbasiyadkori2011improved}; frozen evaluation removes the optimism term.  This division parallels learned node or branching selection in exact combinatorial optimization~\cite{mattick2023node}, but the transition system here includes exact quantum lowering, semantic canonicalization, ancilla contracts, and an independent circuit certifier.
