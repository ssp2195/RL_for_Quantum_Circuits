\section{Related Work}

Exact Clifford+$T$ synthesis is governed by algebraic restrictions on the representable unitary matrices. Giles and Selinger characterize exact multiqubit Clifford+$T$ synthesis with local ancillas and distinguish it from the no-ancilla case~\cite{giles2013exact}; Kliuchnikov develops related exact-synthesis foundations~\cite{kliuchnikov2013synthesis}. The present work does not replace those constructive characterizations. It studies how a finite exact search can be represented, scheduled, pruned, and certified once a native grammar and workspace contract are fixed.

The role of workspace follows the standard reversible-computation model. A reversible network may compute a classical predicate into temporary qubits, use that information through control or phase kickback, and apply the inverse computation to erase garbage~\cite{nielsen2010quantum}. Clean ancillas carry a known initial state, whereas borrowed ancillas carry an arbitrary input that must be restored. The requirement is coherent: it must hold on superpositions and, for borrowed registers, when the borrowed subsystem is entangled with an external reference. This motivates the isometry formulation used below rather than testing only computational-basis examples.

Clifford transformations admit compact tableau or binary symplectic representations~\cite{aaronson2004improved,bravyi2021hadamard}. Clifford-conjugated $T$ gates can be represented as $\pi/4$ rotations about Pauli operators, including operators with support on several logical or workspace wires~\cite{zhang2019optimizing,vandaele2023optimal}. The current state retains both forward and inverse Pauli actions so that a $T$-rotation axis can be transported through the accumulated Clifford frame without replaying the complete prefix. The strengthened canonicalizer is conservative: it applies exact signed-axis normalization, commuting reordering, equal-axis fusion, and Clifford extraction, but it does not claim a complete normal form for arbitrary noncommuting odd Pauli rotations.

Learning has been applied to gate-level circuit construction, transpilation, ZX-calculus rewriting, Pauli-network synthesis, and symmetry-aware Clifford reduction~\cite{kolle2024environment,kremer2024practical,riu2023zx,dubal2025pauli,yeung2026equivariant}. Learned residual-cost estimates have also been combined with explicit search~\cite{theissinger2026beyond}. The present action semantics are narrower. Learning schedules units of a deterministic exact process: outer SARSA chooses a frontier record, and a linear contextual bandit chooses which pending exact continuations of that record receive computation. This separation is analogous to learned node or branching selection in exact combinatorial optimization~\cite{mattick2023node}, while retaining a quantum-specific semantic archive and independent circuit verifier.

LinUCB supplies an interpretable linear contextual-bandit rule with a closed-form ridge estimate and confidence bonus~\cite{li2010contextual,abbasiyadkori2011improved}. It is used only during inner-policy training. Frozen evaluation removes the optimism term and ranks continuations by posterior mean. The outer controller remains semi-gradient SARSA because selecting a frontier record changes the future frontier and therefore has delayed consequences~\cite{sutton2018rl}. Potential-based shaping is used in a form that preserves the finite-horizon terminal objective~\cite{ng1999policy}.

Finally, independent equivalence checking is essential when a symbolic normalizer is intentionally incomplete~\cite{burgholzer2020advanced}. In the present small-register qualification, the final verifier propagates the persistent witness densely on only the permitted input columns. This remains exponential in total width but is smaller than constructing a full target unitary and, more importantly, enforces the correct logical/workspace contract.
